About

The Dog Rescue Interface offers a dog rescue-shelter search feature, in promotion of accessible online advocacy in support of the dog rescue community. We want easy access to locations and contact information of rescue groups. Implementing a collective approach to educate and inform the public nationwide about the dog rescue community and how to stay involved.
A place to be proactive. The middle man who is meant to fill in the cracks.
Search for local and non-local rescues who need advocacy. This helps our dog shelters maintain space. Attention attracts success in adoptions and fundraising needs.
Your search results will include straight forward information on locations and social media accounts, where you can reach out, share stories, and offer support. Many rescue groups operate heavily on social media where they share updates, fundraising campaigns, and urgent needs for shelter dogs awaiting for fosters and adoptions.
