About

The Dog Rescue Interface offers a dog rescue-shelter search feature, in promotion of accessible online advocacy in support of the dog rescue community. We want easy access to locations and contact information of rescue groups. Implementing a collective approach to educate and inform the public nationwide about the dog rescue community and how to stay involved.
A place to be proactive. The middle man who is meant to fill in the cracks.
Search for local and non-local rescues and dogs who need advocacy. This helps our dog shelters maintain space, and it helps to expose adoptable dogs looking for homes. Attention attracts success in adoptions and fundraising needs.
Your search results will include locations and contact information, so that you can reach out, share stories to social media, and offer support. Many rescue groups operate heavily on social media where they share updates, fundraising campaigns, and urgent needs for shelter dogs awaiting for fosters and adoptions. You can also search by zip code any community discussion, and even search for breeds that need exposure.
